Richardson (`microservices-patterns-richardson.md`, Key Terminology) and Hard Parts (`software-architecture-hard-parts-ford.md`, Key Terminology) both flag the gap. Related candidates from the same files: `Pivot Transaction`, `Static Coupling` / `Dynamic Coupling`. The Hard Parts saga archetype names (Epic Saga, Horror Story, ...) are deliberately not proposed: they are memory aids, not product-owner vocabulary.

**Decision (2026-11-19, product owner, during #1406):** defer. The `workflow-orchestration` rebuild does not touch CLAUDE.md or `glossary-management`; it uses the terms as they stand. Take this up in the Wave 2 housekeeping step (end of #1376), once every Wave 2 skill's final vocabulary is known, so the glossary is changed once.
