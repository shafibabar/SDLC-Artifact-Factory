# D046: One acceptance sign-off per release; beta stage moves are graduation records, and a conditional sign-off may reach 100% (#1348, W1-43)

**Date:** 2026-11-01

**Rationale:** The Wave 1 audit (W1-43) found the number of sign-offs per release undefined: `beta-program-design` asked for a conditional sign-off to enter open beta and a full one for GA, while the requirements-analyst produced one sign-off at the end and `phase-requirements.json` requires one sign-off file. The product owner chose one sign-off per release, at the close of Customer Validation. Its rollout action states the whole path to GA; beta stage moves (closed → open beta → GA) are `beta-program-design` graduation records, not further sign-offs; a conditional sign-off may take the release to 100% with its conditions tracked. Customer Validation re-opens, with a new UAT pass and a superseding sign-off, only when widening surfaces a new Critical or High, a graduation fails, or a representative withdraws. At enterprise, each customer representative accepts for their own tenant cohort; a refused cohort is held back and its later acceptance is an addendum to the same record. ISTQB treats alpha/beta as a form of acceptance testing, which supports one acceptance decision (Hambling research file).

**Alternatives rejected:**
- A sign-off per widening stage (open beta, then a full one at GA) — more records, a second two-party agreement per stage, and a conditional could never reach 100%.
