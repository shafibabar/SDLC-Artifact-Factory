# D045: Fresh-Eyes Wave 1 design decisions — strategy kernel replaces the mission statement, vision → kernel → OKRs → roadmap, MoSCoW finalises a provisional priority, a solo product owner may sign off UAT (#1292)

**Date:** 2026-10-19

**Rationale:** The Wave 1 audit (`discovery/fresh-eyes/wave-1-findings.md`, #1293) and 15 new research files (#1295, #1297–#1310) surfaced cross-skill contradictions that need one answer each. The product owner decided:
- **Strategy order:** vision → strategy kernel → OKRs → roadmap. The vision alone owns the North Star metric. product-strategist's run order changes to match (Doerr, Lombardo, Rumelt).
- **Mission statement → strategy kernel:** `mission-statement` is replaced by a `strategy-kernel` artifact (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions), required at every scale; the mission's present-tense line folds into the vision. Rumelt finds no support for a separate mission and names goals-as-strategy as bad strategy.
- **MoSCoW:** epics and stories carry a provisional priority; `moscow-prioritization` finalises it. The 60% person-week capacity rule is dropped for structural checks; Must = DSDM's Minimum Usable SubseT = the story map's walking skeleton. Lean gets a short Must / Won't-this-time block on the backlog.
- **Ideate order:** requirements-analyst writes the impact map (where required) before the functional requirements.
- **Lean profile:** `business-model-canvas` becomes required at lean as a Lean Canvas (Maurya).
- **UAT and sign-off:** a solo product owner may run UAT and sign off, recorded as an exception (ISTQB independence "none"); a full sign-off is allowed. A conditional sign-off may carry open High defects only with a fix plan; one rule, stated in `acceptance-sign-off`, that uat-plan, uat-scenario and the agent point to.
- **Product-specific rules** (mandatory Data Steward / Compliance Officer personas, SOC 2 checks in the beta validator) move into examples; examples span several products and scales.
- **NFRs:** targets scale with the profile (minimum at lean, target at standard, stretch at enterprise); DORA delivery figures are process targets owned by `dora-metrics`, not product quality attributes.
- **New skills:** `opportunity-solution-tree` and `pricing-and-packaging` (Wave 1); `architecture-evaluation` (Wave 2, architecture domain).

**Alternatives rejected:**
- Merge the mission into the vision only — leaves no artifact holding the strategy.
- Keep the mission and add a kernel — most artifacts, least research support.
- Cap a solo sign-off at conditional — rejected by the product owner; the exception record carries the risk instead.
- Keep the 60% Must rule — needs person-week estimates an agent-built product never has.
