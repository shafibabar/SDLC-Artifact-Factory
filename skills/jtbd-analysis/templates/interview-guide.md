# Interview Guide — <topic>

**Purpose:** <the job, outcome or force these conversations should shed light on>
**Interviewer:** <the user>. The agent wrote this guide and will turn your notes into a snapshot afterwards; it does not take part in the conversation.
**Framing to use when inviting:** "I'm trying to understand how <people like you> handle <the situation>." Not "feedback on our product".

## Whom to talk to

| Criterion | Why |
|---|---|
| Recently switched how they do <the job> (bought, built, hired or dropped something) | Remembers the timeline |
| Already spent time or money on the problem (earlyvangelist) | A past action, not an opinion |
| <segment / tenant role> | <coverage gap this fills> |

## Opening (one prompt)

"Tell me about the last time you <situation>, from the moment <start>."

## Timeline probes (switch interview) <!-- or Excavation probes (story-based): what happened first, then what, who else, what was hardest -->

| Stage | Ask |
|---|---|
| First thought | "When did you first think there had to be a better way? What had just happened?" |
| Passive looking | "Before you went looking, what had you noticed or heard about?" |
| Event → active looking | "What happened that made you actually start looking?" |
| Active looking | "What did you look at or try? What did you rule out, and why?" |
| Event → deciding | "Why then, and not a month later?" |
| Deciding | "What nearly stopped you? Who else had a say? What did you give up?" |
| First use | "What happened the first day? When did you know it was working, or not?" |
| Ongoing use / firing | "What brings you back in an ordinary week?" or "Why did you stop?" |

**When they generalise** ("I usually…"): "And that last time, what happened?"
**When they pitch an idea** ("you should build…"): "What would that let you do? When did you last need that?"

## The question I'm afraid to ask

<!-- Still about the present or a past event: never "would you…" or "if X went away tomorrow…". -->

"<Is this a priority this quarter? When did you last spend money or time on it, and who signed off?>"

"<Could I see the spreadsheet you use? / Who else should I talk to? / Could we look at your next month-end together?>"
