# GTM Strategy Checklist — Run Before Hand-Off

Self-contained. Cross-check every GTM strategy against this list before presenting it to the user. Each row is a hard stop: a GTM that fails a row goes back with the failing row named. Rows marked *(enterprise)* apply only at enterprise. At lean the artifact is skipped; a rollout note written on request is checked only against rows 1, 9 and 12.

| # | Check | How to test it | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kernel carried out** | Does the beachhead match the kernel's focus? Is every chosen segment and channel allowed by the kernel's rules-out list? | A ruled-out segment or channel; no reference to the kernel |
| 2 | **Beachhead scored** | Are candidates scored on the six criteria, with WTP evidence and kernel fit, before the ICP? | One segment asserted; the biggest market chosen by size alone |
| 3 | **ICP is an organisation** | Are the attributes organisational, with ≥2 disqualifiers and one anchored in an observed customer (or labelled hypothesis)? | Persona traits; no disqualifiers |
| 4 | **Buying group** | For a B2B purchase: roles with belief, objection, proof, likely profile; a Mobilizer hypothesis and a Blocker; where consensus is built | A single "buyer"; profiles stated as fact; no buying group in a self-serve B2B product that passes procurement |
| 5 | **Positioning from the competitive analysis** | Are the alternatives cited from the competitive analysis (doing nothing / no decision included)? Are the unique attributes its filtered points, with evidence status? | A second alternatives list; "unlike other tools"; a hypothesis presented as proven; positioning derived from the vision or a mission |
| 6 | **Category chosen** | Is the category fit / subcategory / create, with the rejected option and why? | A category label filled in with no choice made |
| 7 | **Reframe and message house** | Is there a Reframe line that only this product's strength answers? Is the customer the protagonist? Are proof points verifiable; is any customer story real and named? | No reframe; adjectives as proof; an invented customer story |
| 8 | **One primary channel** | One primary channel reaching the whole beachhead; ≤2 supporting; deferrals with a revisit point | Four or more channels in parallel |
| 9 | **Pricing** | Model, charging unit (three tests) and packaging stated separately; value anchor; WTP labelled stated / committed; strategy named and justified; a range per tier; the four-failure check run. Lean: one line, or "not monetised" with the reason | "Set in the commercial plan"; cost-plus; penetration with no lock-in; a price with no evidence |
| 10 | **Packaging detail** *(enterprise)* | Leaders / fillers / killers per segment; fences; discount policy with give-get | One tier list for every segment; ad-hoc discounting |
| 11 | **Exit criteria on evidence** | Every stage has measurable exits; Stage 1 rests on Mom-Test evidence, records WTP and the Mobilizer test; Stage 2 has price acceptance | Durations only; "partners loved it" |
| 12 | **Metrics** | Every metric has a target or a stated setting point; no unsourced benchmark; no-decision losses counted | "Benchmark 15–25%" with no source; metrics with no targets and no plan |
| 13 | **Chasm kept separate** | Launch stages and the chasm diagnostic are separate checks; GA is not called "crossing the chasm" | "We reached GA, so we crossed the chasm" |
| 14 | **Shape and scale** | Frontmatter has `name`, `version`, `phase`, `owner`, `created`, `summary`; size matches the skill's Scale table | Missing `summary`; enterprise sections in a standard artifact or the reverse |

## Fast triage order

1. Row 5 (positioning not from the competitive analysis): everything downstream inherits it.
2. Row 9 (pricing with no evidence, or deferred).
3. Rows 2 and 1 (beachhead not chosen, or chosen against the kernel).
4. Rows 4 and 11 (no buying group; exits on goodwill).
5. The rest.
