# Exploratory Session Record Template

Copy one block per session, under the UAT plan's Exploratory Charters, or into the feedback log when the product keeps one. Fill it during and straight after the session, never later from memory. How to write the charter and pick heuristics: `../references/exploratory-testing.md`.

```markdown
### EX-[n] — [short title]
**Charter:** Explore [target], with [resources], to discover [information].
**Time-box:** [45–90 min] · **Explorer:** [a human: name and role] · **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Environment:** [same as the scripted scenarios: environment, version / digest, flags]
**Heuristics used:** [CRUD / boundaries / Goldilocks / interruptions / configuration / saboteur]
**Notes (taken during the session):**
- [time] [action] → [observation]
**Defects and issues:** [FB-n (severity), routed to feedback-template] or "none"
**New charters surfaced:** [Explore …] or "none"
**Debrief:** [what was learned, in two or three sentences]
```
