# Willingness-to-Pay Conversation Guide — Template

Self-contained. The agent fills this in and hands it to the user, who runs it with prospective customers or design partners (the agent cannot hold the conversations). Replace every `[bracketed]` value. Keep it to about 20 minutes. The questions follow Ramanujam and Tacke, *Monetizing Innovation*; the "past first" opening follows Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*. Record answers in the artifact's WTP Evidence table, labelled **stated**; only a commitment at the end upgrades a figure to **committed**.

---

```markdown
# WTP conversation — [product], [segment]

**Who to ask:** [role — the person who holds the budget, not only the user]
**Record:** role, company size band, date. No names in the artifact.

## 1. Their life first (5 min)
- How do you handle [the job] today? What did it cost you last [month / quarter] — time, money, write-offs?
- What have you already paid for or tried to fix it?

## 2. The offer (2 min)
Read this description, unchanged in every conversation:
> [What it does, for whom, what it replaces. No price.]

## 3. Price range (5 min)
- At what price per [charging unit] would this be **acceptable**?
- At what price would it be **expensive**, but you'd still consider it?
- At what price would it be **too expensive** to consider?

## 4. What matters (5 min)
- Split 100 points across: [benefit 1], [benefit 2], [benefit 3], [benefit 4], [benefit 5].
- Is anything here that would make you **less** likely to buy? Why?

## 5. Likelihood at real prices (2 min)
- At [price A] per [unit], how likely are you to buy (not at all / maybe / very)? At [price B]?
- What would have to change for you to buy?

## 6. Ask for a commitment (1 min)
- [A paid pilot at price A / a letter of intent / a pre-order]. Would you do that now?
  Record the answer exactly: yes (committed), a next step on the calendar, or no.
```
