We're in the Strategy phase of the SDLC Artifact Factory (standard profile), writing the Pricing Model section of the GTM strategy for **Tutorbase**, scheduling and invoicing software for independent tutoring centres (2–15 tutors).

What we have:
- 8 interviews with centre owners. Asked about a described offer, they said £30–60 a month is acceptable, £90 is expensive, and over £150 they wouldn't consider it.
- 2 centres signed letters of intent at £49 a month.
- Owners say invoicing and chasing parents takes about 5 hours a week, at roughly £25 an hour.
- Feature ranking from the interviews: invoicing and parent payment links first, scheduling second; the parent mobile app ranked last, and 3 owners said a parent app would mean "more support calls".

My co-founder's draft: "Launch at £19/month to grab market share fast. Two tiers: Basic, and Pro with everything including the parent app. Per-tutor pricing. Exact prices will be set later in the commercial plan. Target trial-to-paid of 15–25%, the PLG benchmark."
