Using this plugin's gtm-strategy skill, write the go-to-market strategy artifact (in your reply, not to a file) for this standard-profile product. Pricing can be brief; focus on the segment, the buyers, the positioning, the messaging and the launch.

"**Fleetcheck**: daily vehicle-inspection checklists on drivers' phones for trucking firms, with defects routed to the workshop the same hour.

- Strategy kernel (approved): crux, defects reported on paper reach the workshop days late, so trucks fail roadside inspections. Guiding policy: concentrate on owner-run haulage firms of 10–80 trucks and make the driver's check faster than the paper form; relies on a 90-second check that works offline. Rules out fleets over 200 trucks and telematics hardware.
- Competitive analysis (approved): alternatives A1 doing nothing (paper forms in the cab, filed weekly), A2 no decision (the owner agrees but the transport manager won't change the drivers' routine), A3 a spreadsheet the transport manager fills from paper, A4 the inspection module in two telematics suites (sold with hardware). Differentiation: D1 the 90-second offline check (real and valued; verified in 4 pilot fleets), D2 same-hour defect routing to the workshop (real and valued; copyable).
- Win/loss log: 3 pilot fleets converted; 2 fleets stalled with no decision (transport manager objected to changing the drivers' routine).
- Canvas: subscription per vehicle per month; 3 pilot fleets pay £6 per vehicle; 7 interviews stated £4–9 acceptable.
- Stakeholder map: the owner pays; drivers do the checks; the transport manager runs compliance and the workshop.
- Candidate segments the team has discussed: owner-run haulage firms of 10–80 trucks; all UK commercial fleets including vans; large logistics groups (200+ trucks)."
