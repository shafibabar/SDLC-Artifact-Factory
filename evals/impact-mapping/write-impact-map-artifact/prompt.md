Linked Key Result KR-3.1: "Share of booked rooms actually used, from 62% to 85% by 2027-06-30" (measured by door-sensor check-ins, all tenant campuses, rolling 4 weeks).

Our approved opportunity solution tree (`ideate/opportunity-solution-tree.md`) has KR-3.1's metric as its product outcome.
