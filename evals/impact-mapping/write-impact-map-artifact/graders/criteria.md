The artifact satisfies ALL of the following:
1. It begins with a frontmatter block containing at least name, version, phase, owner, created and a one-sentence summary, with phase "ideate" (not "strategy"); and its goal is given as separate fields (at least metric, baseline 62%, target 85% and the 2027-06-30 deadline, ideally also how it is measured), taken from KR-3.1 without invented numbers.
2. Between each impact (behaviour change) and its deliverables, the map shows the customer opportunity behind the impact, and the impact on the chosen path cites the tree's target opportunity OPP-1.2; the tree's solutions SOL-1, SOL-2 and SOL-3 all appear on the map as deliverable candidates (selected or option), at least one of them under the impact that cites OPP-1.2.
3. Each deliverable row is marked as on the selected path or as an option, with an assumption and an observable signal; there is no Must / Should / Could / Won't (MoSCoW) or other priority column.
4. It names at least one blocking actor who can obstruct the goal (for example department heads protecting booked hours as an entitlement), lists the selected path's assumptions labelled by type using desirability, viability, feasibility, usability and ethical, including at least one ethical assumption, and includes at least one non-software deliverable.
Fail if any of the four is missing.
