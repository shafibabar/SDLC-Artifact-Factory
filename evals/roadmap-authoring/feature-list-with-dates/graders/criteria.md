The roadmap artifact satisfies ALL of the following:
1. A frontmatter block with name, version, phase, owner, created AND a one-sentence summary field.
2. A disclaimer as its own section (stating the roadmap reflects current understanding and will change), plus a one-line reference to the vision or its North Star and a block listing the Objectives / Key Results the roadmap serves.
3. The rows are themes phrased as customer or user problems (e.g. "patients forget their appointments"), not feature names like "SMS reminders" or "Insurer claims export"; each row names the Key Result (e.g. KR1.1, KR2.1) or Objective it serves (a Next or Later row may instead cite the guiding-policy clause it carries out).
4. Concrete solutions/features (SMS reminders, claims export) appear only as candidate solutions in the Now horizon; Next and Later rows carry no solutions. Every row has a confidence level, and confidence is lower in Later than in Now.
5. No delivery date is attached to any item beyond Now, EXCEPT the 2027-03-01 insurer-claims date, which is kept and explicitly marked as committed (tied to the committed OKR / contract). Broad timeframe labels (Now/Next/Later, a quarter or half as a horizon heading), the artifact's own created/revision dates, and draft dates quoted only to identify a rejected item are not delivery dates.
6. A "Not On This Roadmap" (or equivalently named) section lists the multi-site chain dashboard and the patient exercise app, each with a reason that cites the strategy kernel's guiding policy (what it rules out).
Fail if any of the six is missing.
