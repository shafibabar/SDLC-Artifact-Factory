We're in the Strategy phase of the SDLC Artifact Factory (enterprise profile) for **Fieldnote**, inspection-report software for building-surveying firms, charged per surveyor per month. The approved GTM strategy sets the price strategy as maximisation and a price range of £45–75 per surveyor per month.

What we have:
- 10 interviews with firm owners about a described offer: £40–80 per surveyor per month acceptable, £120 expensive, over £180 they wouldn't consider it.
- 3 firms have paid for a 3-month pilot at £55 per surveyor per month.
- Surveyors say report writing takes about 4 hours a week, at roughly £45 an hour.
- Our cost to serve is about £12 per surveyor per month; fixed build-and-run cost is about £300,000 a year.
- A professional directory lists 4,200 firms with 3–20 surveyors (average 6).

My draft:
"**Price:** cost to serve £12 + 50% margin = £18 per surveyor per month, so we undercut everyone.
**Business case:** the UK surveying software market is worth £1.2B; if we capture 1% that's £12M a year."
