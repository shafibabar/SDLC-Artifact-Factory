We're in the Strategy phase of the SDLC Artifact Factory (standard profile) for **Rotaly**, staff-rota scheduling software for restaurants. Our approved GTM says: subscription, charged per location per month, maximisation, three tiers.

Interview evidence (14 owners and operations managers):
- Single-site cafés and restaurants (9 interviews) ranked **auto-scheduling from forecast covers** first and **shift-swap in the staff app** second. Four of them said a mandatory **payroll export set-up** at onboarding "would put us off — our accountant does payroll".
- Multi-site groups (5 interviews, 4–30 locations) ranked **cross-location staff sharing** and a **group labour-cost dashboard** first; auto-scheduling second. All five need the payroll export.
- Nobody ranked custom colour themes, PDF rota printing or priority email support.
