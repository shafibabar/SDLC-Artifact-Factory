The artifact satisfies ALL of the following:
1. It begins with a frontmatter block containing at least name, version, phase, owner, created and a one-sentence summary, and the phase is "ideate" (not "strategy" or "discovery").
2. Each core job has a one-line job statement in verb + object + clarifier form (not in "When…, I want to…" form), and beneath it job stories in "When…, I want to…, so I can…" form; the two formats are labelled as different things (job statement vs job story).
3. Every job story cites at least one of the three interviews by its ID (e.g. 2026-10-06-l-1 or l-1) or is explicitly marked as an assumption.
4. It includes a forces of progress breakdown naming push, pull, anxiety and habit; a list of what gets fired (the alternatives replaced) that includes doing nothing / non-consumption (the landlord who tracks nothing); and a Big Hire versus Little Hire distinction (what wins the purchase versus what brings the user back in ordinary use).
Fail if any of the four is missing.
