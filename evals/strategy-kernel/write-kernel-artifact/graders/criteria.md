The artifact satisfies ALL of the following:
1. It has a frontmatter block that includes name, version, phase, owner, created AND a one-sentence summary field.
2. It has three labelled parts: Diagnosis, Guiding Policy and Coherent Actions. The diagnosis names one specific crux (a single challenge), not a list of goals or numbers, and states or shows that other challenges were set aside.
3. The guiding policy names the advantage or leverage it relies on (linked to the competitive analysis, e.g. reading the shop's own sales history / special-order notes) AND an explicit "rules out" list of at least two directions, each with a reason.
4. The coherent actions (3–6) each state which other action or which part of the policy they reinforce.
5. It includes a review trigger: the evidence that would show the diagnosis is wrong.
6. It hands the OKR step proximate objectives (or equivalent) and does not state numeric growth targets such as revenue or customer counts as the strategy.
Fail if any of the six is missing.
