The Platform vision artifact is explicitly tied to the parent product vision: it names the parent vision (or links to it) AND states which part of the parent's future it serves or why the parent depends on it, AND it has a north star metric that is stated to feed or roll up into the parent's north star (share of tenants whose estate is continuously provable). The artifact must also contain a "rules out" list (directions the vision says no to) that does not contradict the parent (e.g. it does not rule in processing data outside the tenant's region). Missing any of: the explicit parent link, the roll-up of the north star, or the rules-out list, fails.
