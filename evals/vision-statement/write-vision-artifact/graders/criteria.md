The artifact satisfies ALL of the following:
1. The vision statement itself is at most 60 words and is written with the users (nurses / nursing units / charge nurses) as the subject or protagonist, not the app.
2. It does NOT use the positioning template: no "For … who …, [product] is a …" construction and no "Unlike [competitor]" clause.
3. It describes a future state (what is true for nurses or patients when the product succeeds), not a feature list of what the app does.
4. It includes an explicit section listing what the vision rules out: at least two directions the product will not pursue.
5. It names a north star metric implied by the vision.
Fail if any of the five is missing.
