We're in the SDLC Artifact Factory (standard profile) for **Clinicflow**, scheduling software for independent physiotherapy clinics. Our approved competitive analysis names one differentiation point that the strategy kernel's guiding policy relies on: "D1 — automatic waitlist backfill: a cancelled slot is offered to waitlisted patients by text within a minute."

The closed beta with 8 clinics just ended:
- 3 clinics converted to paid, saying the backfill "filled slots we'd have lost".
- 2 went back to their paper diary and phone, saying "we fill gaps by ringing our regulars anyway".
- 2 never decided: the owner wanted it, the senior physio didn't want patients texted automatically, and the trial lapsed.
- 1 chose the waitlist feature built into the practice-management software it already pays for.

Using this plugin's competitive-analysis guidance, what do we do with these results, and which of our strategy artifacts change?
