The response satisfies ALL of the following:
1. It replaces the "same origin, destination and weight in five minutes" heuristic with an idempotency key the caller sends with each `TenderShipment` (reused on every retry of the same request), and says a duplicate gets back the original outcome (the same shipment and result) instead of a second tender or an error. It points out that the heuristic both merges genuine separate loads that look alike and misses retries that arrive after five minutes (wave-2-findings, command-catalog § Examples (3): "an idempotent `CapturePayment` retried across a regional failover"; implementing-ddd-vernon, Core Concepts: at-least-once delivery "means the same event may be processed more than once", so handling must be idempotent; api-design-patterns-geewax, Applies To row for command-catalog: the catalog carries the command's "idempotency design").
