The backlog satisfies ALL of the following:
1. Because at the lean profile the backlog is also the functional requirements, it contains a requirements section (for example a "Requirements Header") with the scope, the choir's policies written as numbered business rules (for example BR-1 one copy per singer per piece; BR-2 due back the week after the concert), and numbered transition items (for example TR-1) for replacing the paper sign-out book (such as entering the ~80 copies currently out, and retiring or archiving the paper book).
2. Stories carry a provisional priority (for example high / medium / low), not Must / Should / Could on each story, and no story points or sprint estimates; the release's Musts are settled instead in a closing Must / Won't-this-time block (or equivalently named block) at the end of the backlog, where each Won't has a reason or workaround.
3. Every story names a persona or role (singer / librarian, or P-1 / P-2) rather than "a user", and traces by ID to a job story (JS-1.1 or JS-2.1) and to KR-1.1.
Fail if any of the three is missing.
