The artifact satisfies ALL of the following:
1. It has a frontmatter block that includes name, version, phase, owner, created AND a one-sentence summary field, and it is a Lean Canvas (Problem, Solution, Key Metrics and Unfair Advantage blocks), not a full nine-block canvas with Key Partnerships, Key Activities and Key Resources.

3. EVERY block carries an explicit evidence status (for example hypothesis / tested / validated, or an equivalent per-block label), and the £2-a-month replies are NOT treated as validated: they are labelled as stated interest, a hypothesis or at most tested.

6. The Revenue Streams block names what is charged per (the charging unit, e.g. per swimmer per month) and the willingness-to-pay evidence, distinguishing stated interest from a commitment such as an actual payment.
